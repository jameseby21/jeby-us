Research · Carnegie Mellon University, 2015

Predicting the masses of galaxy clusters.

My 2015 undergraduate poster at Carnegie Mellon, rebuilt so the figures move. The short version: the standard calibration counted each cluster about ten times, and counting each one honestly makes clusters a few percent lighter.
