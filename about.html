The story so far

How I got here.

The short version, in five parts.

  1. High school

    The math club

    It started with the math club. In 2013 I took sixth place in statistics at the Mu Alpha Theta national convention.

  2. 2015 – 2019

    Carnegie Mellon

    I studied statistics, with a concentration in computer science. My undergraduate research asked whether the usual way of weighing galaxy clusters counted the same data more than once. It did. I was president of the archery club, and I taught kids to code at a summer camp.

  3. 2019 – 2025

    Work

    Six years in business intelligence, the last of them leading analytics at a startup. The details are on the Professional page.

  4. 2025

    Then my health stepped in

    In 2025 the startup ran out of money while I was in the hospital, and the job went with it.

    I had open-heart surgery.

    I couldn't choose that part. I could choose what to do with the time I had.

  5. Since then

    I kept building

    I build things in my spare time: a real-estate database, a learning library, keyboard lighting software, a band game, and a houseplant that roasts your friends. Some are out and some are still ideas; the Lab says which. I also tutor math, statistics and data. Drew handles morale.
