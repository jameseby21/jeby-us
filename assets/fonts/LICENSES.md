# Fonts served from this site

Every font here is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows self-hosting and redistribution. The full licence text for each family sits next to the files (OFL-*.txt). Files are the Latin subset as WOFF2, taken from the Google Fonts distribution on 2026-11-18 (W26, decision 210). `instrument-sans-ext.woff2` is Google's latin-ext slice of Instrument Sans cut down to one glyph, U+0237 (the dotless j of the wordmark), because the Latin slice lacks it. Each site is published with only its own families' files and licence texts.
