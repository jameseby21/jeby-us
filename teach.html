Questions

Online or in person?

Both. Online sessions run on video with a shared whiteboard. In-person sessions are in Miami.

English or Spanish?

Either, or both in the same session. Clases en español disponibles.

How do I pay?

Through a secure payment link before the session. Packs are paid up front and don't expire for six months.

What's the cancellation policy?

Reschedule for free up to 24 hours ahead. Later cancellations count as a session.

Do you work with younger students?

Yes. For students under 18, a parent books, stays in the loop, and nothing is recorded without written consent.
